Independent product case study · Wealth SaaS 1/5

Problem: wealth data is fragmented, stale and hard to trust.

Fragmented

Holdings sit across brokers, banks, private funds and spreadsheets—often across currencies.

Unreconciled

PDF data is re-keyed manually; users struggle to know which number is authoritative.

Advice without trust

Recommendations built on bad data destroy confidence faster than no recommendation at all.

Anchor principle: trust is a product capability, not a compliance footnote.

Payments synthesis

The strongest fintech products compound value after the payment.

1 · Acceptance

Make it easy to take money across channels and rails.

2 · Trust

Confirm success, explain failures and make settlement visible.

3 · Operations

Reconciliation, refunds, reports and exceptions retain merchants.

4 · Expansion

Data + trust unlock affordability, credit and adjacent software.

My PM lens: a payment is not the endpoint—it is the first reliable event in a longer financial workflow.
